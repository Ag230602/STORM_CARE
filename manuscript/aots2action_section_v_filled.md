## V-B. RQ1: Calibration, Sharpness, and Track Error

For the primary 48 h endpoint, the selected P90 representation is the P90 covariance ellipse. At 48 h, its empirical P90 coverage is 0.9880 with a 95% bootstrap confidence interval of [0.9635, 1.0000]. The corresponding mean region area is 103,430.49 km2. Relative to the P90 percentile cone comparison method, whose mean area is 158,047.33 km2 at the same lead time, the P90 covariance ellipse reduces mean area by 34.56%.

The ensemble-mean track error increases with lead time, from 36.28 km at 6 h to 208.31 km at 96 h. The P90 covariance ellipse is above nominal coverage at every evaluated horizon: 0.9245 at 6 h, 0.9286 at 12 h, 0.9400 at 24 h, 0.9880 at 48 h, 0.9538 at 72 h, and 0.9400 at 96 h. Thus, the primary representation is sharper than the P90 percentile cone at 48 h while remaining conservative rather than under-covering; the generated results do not support describing it as exactly nominal.

## V-E. RQ4: Scalability

The completed scalability experiment used ensemble sizes M = {5, 10, 20, 40, M_full}, where M_full = 51, and spatial sizes |X| = {286, 716, 1432, 2147, 2863}. Each configuration was repeated 10 times.

At fixed spatial volume, log-log slopes for runtime versus ensemble size range from 0.782 to 0.809 with R2 values from 0.996 to 0.997. At fixed ensemble size, log-log slopes for runtime versus spatial volume range from 0.482 to 0.506 with R2 values from 0.984 to 0.990. Across all configurations, the global runtime slope versus M|X| is 0.654 with R2 = 0.943. These measured proxy-kernel results are therefore not cleanly consistent with O(M|X|), and the observed scaling should not be described as linear.

Peak memory is nearly flat across ensemble size and is dominated by the loaded Python process and proxy dataset. The largest measured peak in the scalability table is 0.0343 GB, and the full configuration peak is 0.0301 GB.

For the largest configuration, M_full = 51 and N_X = 2863, the mean runtime T is 0.005243 s per forecast case, the median runtime is 0.005239 s, and throughput is 27,848,488.75 items/s. This completes within one 6 h forecast cycle under the measured proxy runtime protocol.
